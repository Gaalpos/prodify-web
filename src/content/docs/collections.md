# Collections

<p class="lead">Keep track of books, movies, series, anime and games: what you want to start, what's in progress and what you've finished.</p>

## Adding items

Search for a title and pick the right result. Prodify fills in the cover, creator, year, genres and length from:

| Type | Source | Key needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Books | Open Library | No |
| Series | TVmaze | No |
| Anime | AniList | No |
| Movies | TMDB | Yes, free |
| Games | RAWG | Yes, free |

Add the free TMDB and RAWG keys in **Settings → Connections**. Game covers can also come from Steam, RetroAchievements, libretro and Wikipedia, with a filter to choose the source.

## Status, ratings and favorites

Each item is **Want**, **In progress**, **Done** or **Dropped**, with the wording matched to its type (Reading, Watching, Playing). Rate items from one to five and mark favorites. Home shows what's in progress, or suggests something from your Want list.

## Browsing

Items show as cards in a masonry layout; game covers keep their own shape. Use the **slider** to change the card size, filter by title, creator or genre, and sort by recently added, your own order, title, rating, year or status.

## Goals, stats and quotes

**Goals & stats** sets yearly goals ("read 24 books") and shows your progress. **Quotes** keeps passages you want to remember.

## Games from Steam and RetroAchievements

Link your accounts in **Settings → Connections**:

- **Steam**: a Steam Web API key and your Steam ID (a profile link or custom URL name works too). Prodify imports your games with playtime and achievements.
- **RetroAchievements**: your username and Web API key. Prodify imports the games you've played, with your achievement progress, and keeps them in sync.
