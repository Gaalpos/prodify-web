# Privacy and sync

<p class="lead">Prodify is local first: your data stays on your device unless you choose to sync it, and it's encrypted before it leaves.</p>

## Local account

The default. It's free forever, needs no sign-up and works offline. Everything stays on this device.

## Password and encryption

In **Settings → Account and data** you can:

- **Add a password**. Prodify asks for it when it opens, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd> locks it straight away.
- **Encrypt your files** on disk with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your password. Encrypted files can only be read inside Prodify.
- **Save a recovery key**. If you forget your password, the recovery key unlocks your data. Keep it somewhere safe: without the password or the recovery key, encrypted data can't be recovered.

## Several users

Several people can use Prodify on one computer. Each user has their own data folder and settings, and you switch between them from Settings.

## Prodify Cloud

Prodify Cloud keeps your devices in sync.

1. In setup or in **Settings → Account and data**, choose **Prodify Cloud**.
2. Enter your email address and type the sign-in code you're sent. There's no separate cloud password.
3. Your files are encrypted on your device with your account's key before they're uploaded. The server stores data it can't read.

Sign in on another device with the same account to sync it. Signing out stops syncing on that device; your data stays both there and in the cloud.

## What goes online

Without Prodify Cloud, Prodify only goes online to fetch things you ask for: collection details and covers, exercise photos, the weather, music, and AI answers if you've added a key.
