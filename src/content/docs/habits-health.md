# Habits and Health

<p class="lead">Count the small things you do each day, and keep track of workouts, meditation and how your body is doing.</p>

## Habits

A habit is either **yes/no** ("Read 20 minutes") or a **number** with a unit ("Drink 8 glasses of water"). For each one you choose:

- **Daily** or **weekly**
- A goal of **at least** or **less than** a target, for numbers and weekly habits
- **Healthy** or **unhealthy**, so a habit you want to break is counted the right way round
- An icon or emoji

Check habits off from Habits or from Home. Each habit shows its **streak**, its **success rate over the last 30 days** and a **Year** view. Use **Prev** and **Next** to fill in a day you missed.

### Linked habits

A habit can be linked to **workouts** or **meditation**, so logging a workout or a meditation session checks it off for you.

## Health

Health has four tabs.

### Overview

Today's healthy habits, your mood over the last 14 days, and this week's workouts and meditation at a glance.

### Gym

Log workouts by date, build **routines**, and track the exercises you do. Exercise suggestions come from a catalog bundled with the app, so they work offline in English and Spanish. Exercises it doesn't know are looked up on wger when you're online.

### Meditation

A meditation timer that logs each session, with your minutes today, this week and a streak.

### Body

Keep your **weight**, **sleep** hours and **water** per day.
