# A quick tour

<p class="lead">Prodify is one app with twelve sections. Here's how to get around and make it yours.</p>

## Home

Home is the page Prodify opens on. It greets you by the time of day and shows cards for what matters now:

- **Tasks** due today and **events** coming up
- **Habits** to check off
- **How are you feeling?**: a quick mood check-in, with an optional note that becomes a journal entry
- **Today's question**: a reflection prompt; your answer is saved in Journal → Questions
- **Focus session**: pick up to three tasks and start a timer
- **Clock and weather** for your city
- **In progress**: what you're reading, watching or playing, or a suggestion from your "Want to" list
- **Sounds**: ambient sounds and radio from the Music section

Lay the cards out in **Columns** or as a **Board**, choose how many columns you want, and turn cards on or off in **Settings → Sections and Home**.

## Moving between sections

On a computer the sections sit in a sidebar. Choose its look in **Settings → Appearance**:

- **Sidebar**: a classic list
- **Cards**: colored tiles with counts
- **Icon grid** and **Icons**: compact icon-only versions
- **Top bar**: sections across the top

Drag sections to reorder them, and drag the sidebar's edge to resize it. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> to auto-hide the sidebar: it slides in when you move the pointer to the left edge of the window.

On a phone, a **tab bar** at the bottom shows Home plus three sections you pick. **More** opens every other section, and **Edit tab bar** changes the three.

## Search and commands

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> anywhere, or use the search box on Home, to search everything: notes, tasks, journal entries, projects, collections and settings. On Home, type <kbd>/</kbd> to run a command instead, such as adding a task for tomorrow. Prodify shows what the command will do before you press Enter, and you can change the command words in Settings.

## Quick capture

The round **+** button in the corner saves something from any page: a task, note, event, link or journal line. **Auto** guesses which one you mean, and task text is read the same way as in [Tasks](../tasks/), so "Pay rent friday" gets a due date.

## Notifications

Reminders appear as system notifications while Prodify is open, and every notification is also kept in the **bell** panel at the top of each page.

## Making it yours

**Settings → Appearance** has light, dark and OLED themes, accent colors, fonts, page width and the navigation layouts. **Settings → General** sets your name, language, time format, first day of the week and the city for the weather. Use the search box at the top of Settings to find any option.
