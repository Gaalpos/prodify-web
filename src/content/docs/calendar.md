# Calendar

<p class="lead">Your events next to everything else that has a date: tasks, journal entries, notes and habits.</p>

## Views

Switch between **Month**, **Week** and **Day**. Colored dots mark what each day holds, following the legend at the top: task, event, journal, note and habit. Pick a day to see all of it underneath.

## Events

An event can be all-day or timed, with an optional end time. Events can repeat **daily**, **weekly**, **monthly** or **yearly**, and weekly events can skip weeks and pick weekdays, for example *every 2 weeks on Mon–Fri* for rotating shifts.

## Birthdays

Add birthdays with an optional birth year. They appear on the calendar every year, with the age they're turning.

## Importing

**Import .ics** brings in events from Google Calendar, Outlook and other apps that export the iCalendar format. One-off events and simple repeats come across.

## Reminders

Events and tasks with a reminder send a notification at their time while Prodify is open.
