# Journal

<p class="lead">A place to write a little every day, with your mood, where you were and what was on your mind.</p>

## Entries

Each entry is its own file with a date, time, mood, location and categories, plus whatever you write. Paste or add photos and they're saved in your `attachments/` folder. **Open in Notes** opens an entry in the full editor.

Entries are made from the **Journal entry** template, so you can change which properties every new entry starts with.

## Mood check-ins

Log how you feel in two taps, from **Low** to **Great**, and add emotions like *Grateful* or *Tired*. You can check in several times a day; a day's mood is the average of its check-ins and entries. The **Emotions** tab shows what you've felt over time.

## Questions

Every day Prodify offers a reflection question, such as "What gave you energy today, and what drained it?". Answers are kept in the **Questions** tab. With AI turned on, new questions can be written for you.

## Categories

Filter the journal by category: Dreams, Family, Fitness, Food, Friends, Goals, Gratitude, Health, Hobbies and more. Add your own with **+**.

## Streak

The streak badge next to the title counts the days in a row you've written. Insights picks highlights from your entries each month.
