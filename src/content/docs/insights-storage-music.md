# Insights, Storage and Music

## Insights

Insights reads your own files and shows a **month in review**. Everything is computed on your device.

- **Average mood** and the emotions you felt most
- **Tasks done**, **workouts** and items **finished** in Collections
- Your **most active day** and **best habits**, with their success over the month
- **Journal highlights** picked from your entries
- Your **focus** sessions

Use the arrows to look back at earlier months.

## Storage

Storage keeps the things you want to come back to:

- **Links**: paste a link to save it, with its title and icon. Add **tags** to group them.
- **Images** and **PDFs**: drop them in to keep a copy in your Prodify folder.
- **Files**: browse every file in your Prodify folder and open it in place.

Show them as a **List** or **Cards**, sort by newest, oldest, A–Z or type, and mark **Favorites**.

## Music

Music plays while you use the rest of the app, controlled from a mini player.

- **Radio**: listener-supported stations from [SomaFM](https://somafm.com), such as Groove Salad, Drone Zone and Deep Space One.
- **Ambient sounds**: rain, ocean waves, wind, fireplace, stream, birds, thunderstorm and café. Mix as many as you like, each with its own volume.

Music needs an internet connection. Ambient recordings are public domain, from [Freesound](https://freesound.org).
