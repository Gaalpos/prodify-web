# Achievements

<p class="lead">Prodify notices what you already do, reading, finishing tasks, keeping habits and learning, and turns it into achievements, XP and levels.</p>

## How it works

There's nothing to set up or log separately. Achievements are counted from your own files every time you open the page: finish a book in Collections or check off a task, and its achievement moves forward.

Each achievement has six **tiers**, and each tier you unlock earns XP:

| Tier | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Bronze | 50 |
| Silver | 100 |
| Gold | 200 |
| Platinum | 350 |
| Diamond | 550 |
| Legend | 800 |

Your XP sets your **level**. Each level needs a little more XP than the one before it. The top of the page shows your level, the XP to the next one and how many tiers you've unlocked. **Almost there** lists the achievements closest to their next tier.

A message pops up when you unlock a new tier.

## The 20 achievements

| Group | Achievements |
| --- | --- |
| Collections | Bookworm (books read), Cinephile (movies watched), Binge watcher (series seasons), Otaku (anime finished), Gamer (games finished), Collector (items in your collections) |
| Tasks and projects | Task master (tasks done), Finisher (projects completed), Deep focus (hours of focus sessions) |
| Habits | Habit builder (habit goals met), Unbroken (longest daily streak) |
| Journal and notes | Journaler (entries), Self-aware (mood check-ins), Curious mind (daily questions answered), Note taker (notes written), Wordsmith (words in notes and journal) |
| Health | Athlete (gym workouts), Mindful (days with meditation) |
| Hobbies and skills | Hobbyist (hobby sessions), Lifelong learner (skill levels gained) |

If you turn a section off, its achievements are hidden too.

## Privacy

Everything is counted on your device from your own files. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there's no leaderboard.
